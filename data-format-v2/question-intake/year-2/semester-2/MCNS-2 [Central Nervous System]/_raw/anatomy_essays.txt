• ANATOMY { CNS 2}
Q1: Mention the musculature derivatives of the first
pharyngeal arch and their nerve supply. (2 Marks)
Muscles of Mastication:
• Masseter
• Temporalis
• Medial pterygoid
• Lateral pterygoid
Other Muscles:
• Tensor tympani
• Tensor veli palatini
• Mylohyoid
• Anterior belly of digastric
Nerve Supply:
• Mandibular branch of the Trigeminal nerve (V3)
Q: Mention the musculature derivatives of the second
pharyngeal arch and their nerve supply.
Muscles: Muscles of facial expression :
• Stapedius, Stylohyoid, and Post. belly of Digastric.
• Nerve: Facial nerve (CN VII).

Q2: Describe the distribution of the Anterior Cerebral
Artery. (2 Marks)
Central Branches:
• Anteromedial set supplying the anterior caudate,
lentiform nucleus, and internal capsule (genu and
anterior limb).
Cortical Branches:
• Medial Surface: Supplies the entire medial surface of the
cerebral hemisphere except the occipital lobe.
• Lateral Surface: Supplies a one-inch strip along the
superior border.
• Inferior Surface: Supplies the medial part of the orbital
surface.
Q3: List the cortical areas supplied by the Anterior
Cerebral Artery (ACA). (2 Marks)
• Medial surface: Supplies the medial surface of the
cerebral hemisphere up to the parieto-occipital fissure,
(excluding the occipital lobe)
• Lateral surface: Supplies a narrow strip along the
superior border of the lateral surface.
• Inferior surface: Supplies the medial part of the orbital
surface of the frontal lobe.

Q4: Mention the blood supply of the Thalamus.
Arterial Supply:
• Medial and Anterior Regions: Posteromedial group of
the Posterior Cerebral Artery (PCA).
• Lateral and Posterior Regions: Posterolateral group of
the PCA (specifically the Thalamogeniculate artery).
Venous Drainage:
• Thalamostriate vein.
Q5: Enumerate the secondary brain vesicles and their
adult derivatives. (3 Marks)
• Telencephalon: Cerebral hemispheres.
• Diencephalon: Thalamus, Hypothalamus, Epithalamus,
and Subthalamus.
• Mesencephalon: Midbrain.
• Metencephalon: Pons and Cerebellum.
• Myelencephalon: Medulla oblongata.

Q6: List the important association areas of the cerebral
cortex. (2 Marks)
• Prefrontal Association Area: Located in the anterior part
of the frontal lobe.
• Parieto-Occipito-Temporal Association Area: Located at
the junction of the parietal., occipital, and temporal
lobes.
• Limbic Association Area: Located in the anterior
temporal pole, ventral frontal lobe, and cingulate gyrus.
Q7: List two anatomical connections carried out by the
Fornix. (1 Mark)
• Connects the Hippocampus to the Mammillary bodies of
the hypothalamus.
• Connects the hippocampus to the septal nuclei and
anterior thalamic nucleus.
Q8: Enumerate the components of the Diencephalon. (1
Mark)
The diencephalon consists of four main parts surrounding the
third ventricle:
• Thalamus
• Hypothalamus
• Epithalamus
• Subthalamus

BONUS
Q: Mention the nerve supply of the third and fourth/sixth
pharyngeal arches.
• 3rd Arch: Glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX).
• 4th & 6th Arches: Vagus nerve (CN X).
Q: Mention the muscular derivative and nerve of the third
pharyngeal arch. (1 Mark)
• Muscle: Stylopharyngeus.
• Nerve: Glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX).
