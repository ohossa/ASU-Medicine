QUESTION_TYPE: essay
SUBJECT: Pathology
CHAPTER: Krabbe Disease
LECTURE: N/A
TEXT: What is Krabbe disease?
EXPLANATION: N/A
KEY_CONCEPT: N/A
MODEL_ANSWER: Krabbe disease is an autosomal recessive disorder characterized by a deficiency of Galactosyl ceramidase, resulting in the accumulation of unmetabolized lipids, leading to demyelination and severe progressive degeneration of motor skills.
