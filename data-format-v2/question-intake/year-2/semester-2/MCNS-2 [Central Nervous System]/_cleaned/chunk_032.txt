*Explanation: Biflagellate trophozoite of Naegleria.*
**Question 107**
30-year-old contact lens user with severe keratitis; culture of tap water would most likely reveal:
a) Acanthamoeba species
b) Entamoeba histolytica
c) Naegleria fowleri
d) Spirometra mansoni
e) Toxoplasma gondii
**Correct Answer: a) Acanthamoeba species**
*Explanation: Acanthamoeba causes keratitis in contact lens users.*
**Question 108**
As regards Acanthamoeba encephalitis:
a) The invasive stage is the amoeboflagellate
b) Trophozoites only can be found in the brain
c) Chronic inflammation of the brain is seen
d) The trophozoite reaches the brain through the cribriform plate
e) It is usually fatal within 10 days
**Correct Answer: c) Chronic inflammation of the brain is seen** (Granulomatous encephalitis)
**Question 109**
A baby born with hydrocephalus (image). Which infection?
a) Congenital toxoplasmosis
b) Neurocysticercosis
c) Granulomatous amoebic encephalitis
d) Amoebic brain abscess
e) Primary amoebic meningoencephalitis
**Correct Answer: a) Congenital toxoplasmosis**
*Explanation: Hydrocephalus, intracranial calcifications, chorioretinitis – classic toxoplasmosis.*
**Question 110**
Treatment of toxoplasmosis in an AIDS patient includes:
a) Albendazole
b) Mebendazole
c) Pyrimethamine
d) Praziquantel
e) Ketoconazole
**Correct Answer: c) Pyrimethamine**
*Explanation: Pyrimethamine + sulfadiazine is standard.*
**Question 1**
Concerning the lateral ventricle, choose one correct answer:
a. Its anterior horn lies in the parietal lobe.
b. Its posterior horn lies in the temporal lobe.
c. It is the cavity of the cerebral hemisphere.
d. Has no choroid plexus.
**Correct Answer: c. It is the cavity of the cerebral hemisphere.**
*Explanation: The lateral ventricles are the cavities of the cerebral hemispheres.*
**Question 2**
The cisterna magna of subarachnoid space is a name given to the:
a. interpeduncular cistern.
b. cistern of the corpus callosum.
c. cistern of the lateral fissure.
d. cerebello‑medullary cistern.
**Correct Answer: d. cerebello‑medullary cistern.**
*Explanation: The cisterna magna is the cerebellomedullary cistern.*
**Question 3**
One of the following, does not belong to the long association fibers:
a. fornix.
b. superior longitudinal bundle.
c. uncinate fasciculus.
d. inferior longitudinal bundle.
**Correct Answer: a. fornix.**
*Explanation: The fornix is a projection fiber (part of the limbic system), not a long association fiber.*
**Question 4**
The paracentral lobule contains motor and sensory areas for one of the following parts:
a. head and neck.
b. trunk.
c. upper limb.
d. lower limb.
**Correct Answer: d. lower limb.**
*Explanation: The paracentral lobule contains the leg and foot area of the primary motor and sensory cortices.*
**Question 5**
One of the following muscles is not inserted into the mandible:
a. temporalis.
b. buccinator.
c. masseter.
d. medial pterygoid.
**Correct Answer: b. buccinator.**
*Explanation: The buccinator inserts into the orbicularis oris and the pterygomandibular raphe, not into the mandible.*
**Question 6**
Regarding the parotid duct, the following statements are correct, except:
a. it courses across masseter below transverse facial artery.
b. it opens into vestibule of mouth opposite upper second molar tooth.
c. it can be palpated against the contracted masseter muscle.
d. it pierces masseter muscle to reach the vestibule of mouth.
**Correct Answer: d. it pierces masseter muscle to reach the vestibule of mouth.**
*Explanation: The parotid duct pierces the buccinator, not the masseter.*
**Question 7**
The following are branches of the mandibular nerve or its divisions, except:
a. nerve to the medial pterygoid from the trunk.
b. superior alveolar nerve from its anterior division.
c. inferior alveolar nerve from its posterior division.
d. auriculo‑temporal nerve from its posterior division.
**Correct Answer: b. superior alveolar nerve from its anterior division.**
*Explanation: The superior alveolar nerve is a branch of the maxillary nerve, not the mandibular.*
**Question 8**
The following nerves are embedded in the lateral wall of the cavernous sinus except:
a. oculomotor.
b. trochlear.
c. ophthalmic division of the trigeminal.
d. mandibular division of the trigeminal.
**Correct Answer: d. mandibular division of the trigeminal.**
*Explanation: The mandibular nerve (V3) does not pass through the cavernous sinus; it exits via foramen ovale.*
**Question 9**
A patient noticed on tongue protrusion it deviates to the left. Possibly this is due to damage of:
a. left lingual nerve.
b. left chorda tympani nerve.
c. left vagus nerve.
d. left hypoglossal nerve.
**Correct Answer: d. left hypoglossal nerve.**
*Explanation: The genioglossus (protrudes tongue) is supplied by the hypoglossal nerve; deviation toward the side of the lesion.*
**Question 10**
The sensory supply of the mucous membrane of the larynx below the level of the vocal cords is:
a. recurrent laryngeal nerve.
b. superior laryngeal nerve.
c. external laryngeal nerve.
d. internal laryngeal nerve.
**Correct Answer: a. recurrent laryngeal nerve.**
*Explanation: Below the vocal folds, sensation is from the recurrent laryngeal nerve (branch of vagus).*
**Question 11**
The only muscle of the soft palate that is not supplied by the vagus nerve is the:
a. palatoglossus.
b. palatopharyngeus.
c. tensor palate.
d. levator palate.
**Correct Answer: c. tensor palate.**
*Explanation: The tensor veli palatini is supplied by the mandibular nerve (V3), not the vagus.*
**Question 12**
Which of the following muscles can retract the mandible?
a. platysma.
b. buccinator.
c. temporalis.
d. masseter.
**Correct Answer: c. temporalis.**
*Explanation: The posterior fibers of the temporalis retract the mandible.*
**Question 13**
One of the followings is a feature of the mandible of old age:
a. The mandible consists of two halves.
b. The mental foramen lies near the upper margin of the mandible.
c. The ramus of the mandible is nearly vertical.
d. The mandibular canal lies near the lower border of the mandible.
**Correct Answer: c. The ramus of the mandible is nearly vertical.**
*Explanation: In old age, after tooth loss, the ramus becomes more vertical and the angle increases.*
**Question 14**
The maxillary nerve leaves the cranial cavity by passing through:
a. Foramen ovale
b. Sphenopalatine foramen
c. Foramen spinosum
d. Foramen rotundum
**Correct Answer: d. Foramen rotundum.**
*Explanation: The maxillary nerve (V2) exits the skull via the foramen rotundum.*
**Question 15**
Regarding the arterial supply of the face, one of the following arteries is a branch of the ophthalmic artery:
a. Dorsal nasal
b. Mental
c. Buccal
d. Intraorbital
**Correct Answer: a. Dorsal nasal.**
*Explanation: The dorsal nasal artery is a terminal branch of the ophthalmic artery.*
**Question 16**
The insertion of which of the following muscles of mastication is close to the wisdom tooth?
a. Masseter
b. Temporalis
c. Lateral pterygoid
d. Medial pterygoid
**Correct Answer: b. Temporalis.**
*Explanation: The temporalis inserts on the coronoid process and anterior border of the ramus, near the third molar.*
**Question 17**
Which of the following structures is embraced by the 2 roots of the auriculotemporal nerve?
a. Superficial temporal artery
b. Posterior auricular artery
c. Middle meningeal artery
d. Accessory meningeal artery
**Correct Answer: c. Middle meningeal artery.**
*Explanation: The auriculotemporal nerve encircles the middle meningeal artery.*
**Question 18**
The Parasympathetic root of sphenopalatine ganglion is:
a. Lesser superficial petrosal nerve
b. Chorda tympani nerve
c. Deep petrosal nerve
d. Greater superficial petrosal nerve
**Correct Answer: d. Greater superficial petrosal nerve.**
*Explanation: The greater petrosal nerve (from facial nerve) carries preganglionic parasympathetics to the pterygopalatine ganglion.*
**Question 19**
The insertion of which of the following muscles prevents an upward enlargement of a thyroid tumor?
a. Sternohyoid
b. Omohyoid
c. Sternothyroid
d. Thyrohyoid
**Correct Answer: c. Sternothyroid.**
*Explanation: The sternothyroid depresses the thyroid cartilage and limits upward movement of the thyroid gland.*
**Question 20**
The 1st cervical spinal nerve communicates with the:
a. Great auricular nerve
b. Hypoglossal nerve
c. Vagus nerve
d. Cranial accessory nerve
**Correct Answer: b. Hypoglossal nerve.**
*Explanation: C1 fibers join the hypoglossal nerve (via the ansa cervicalis).*
**Question 21**
Which one of the following nerves is deep to hyoglossus muscle?
a. Vagus nerve
b. Glossopharyngeal nerve
c. Accessory nerve
d. Hypoglossal nerve
**Correct Answer: b. Glossopharyngeal nerve.**
*Explanation: The glossopharyngeal nerve passes deep to the hyoglossus muscle.*
**Question 22**
The muscles which open the vocal cords are:
a. Posterior cricoarytenoids
b. Lateral cricoarytenoids
c. Thyroarytenoids
d. Transverse arytenoid
**Correct Answer: a. Posterior cricoarytenoids.**
*Explanation: The posterior cricoarytenoids are the only abductors of the vocal cords.*
**Question 23**
Regarding the paleo‑cerebellum, choose the correct statement:
a. It is connected to the vestibular system
b. It is connected to the spinal cord
c. It is connected to the cerebral cortex
d. It is concerned with equilibrium
**Correct Answer: b. It is connected to the spinal cord.**
*Explanation: The paleocerebellum (spinocerebellum) receives input from the spinal cord.*
**Question 24**
The greatest commissure in the brain is:
a. Habenula commissure
b. Hippocampal commissure
c. Corpus callosum
d. Anterior commissure
**Correct Answer: c. Corpus callosum.**
*Explanation: The corpus callosum is the largest commissural fiber tract.*
**Question 25**
Regarding the internal capsule, the auditory radiation passes in:
a. Anterior limb
b. Posterior limb
c. Sublenticular part
d. Retrolenticular part
**Correct Answer: d. Retrolenticular part.**
*Explanation: The auditory radiation (from medial geniculate body) passes through the retrolenticular part of the internal capsule.*
**Question 26**
The main blood supply of the medial surface of the cerebrum is:
a. Posterior inferior cerebellar
b. Anterior cerebral
c. Middle cerebral
d. Posterior cerebral
**Correct Answer: b. Anterior cerebral.**
*Explanation: The anterior cerebral artery supplies the medial surface of the frontal and parietal lobes.*
**Question 27**
The pterion is an important clinical landmark because it overlies the:
a. Superior sagittal sinus
b. Anterior branch of the middle meningeal artery
c. Anterior cerebral artery
d. Confluence of sinuses
**Correct Answer: b. Anterior branch of the middle meningeal artery.**
*Explanation: The pterion overlies the anterior division of the middle meningeal artery.*
**Question 28**
The superficial temporal vein unites with which vein to form the retromandibular vein?
a. Supraorbital
b. Supratrochlear
c. Deep facial
d. Maxillary
**Correct Answer: d. Maxillary.**
*Explanation: The superficial temporal and maxillary veins unite to form the retromandibular vein.*
**Question 29**
The muscle which divides the lacrimal gland into 2 parts is:
a. Levator palpebrae superioris
b. Superior oblique
c. Superior rectus
d. Lateral rectus
**Correct Answer: a. Levator palpebrae superioris.**
*Explanation: The levator palpebrae superioris tendon divides the lacrimal gland into orbital and palpebral parts.*
**Question 30**
The branch of the external carotid artery that passes posterosuperiorly along the inferior border of the posterior belly of the digastric muscle is the:
a. Lingual.
b. Maxillary.
c. Occipital.
d. Posterior auricular.
**Correct Answer: c. Occipital.**
*Explanation: The occipital artery runs along the inferior border of the posterior belly of digastric.*
**Question 31**
The following branch of vagus nerve passes between ECA and ICA:
a. Pharyngeal.
b. Recurrent laryngeal.
c. Superior laryngeal.
d. Meningeal.
**Correct Answer: c. Superior laryngeal.**
*Explanation: The superior laryngeal nerve descends between the internal and external carotid arteries.*
**Question 32**
The choroid plexus of the 4th ventricle is derived from the following artery:
a. Posterior inferior cerebellar.
b. Anterior inferior cerebellar.
c. Superior cerebellar.
d. Middle cerebral.
**Correct Answer: a. Posterior inferior cerebellar.**
*Explanation: The choroid plexus of the 4th ventricle is supplied by the posterior inferior cerebellar artery (PICA).*
**Question 33**
The pyramidal tract fibers supply the following cranial nerve nucleus:
a. Olfactory.
b. Optic.
c. Trigeminal.
d. Vestibulocochlear.
**Correct Answer: c. Trigeminal.**
*Explanation: Corticobulbar fibers supply the motor nucleus of the trigeminal nerve (mastication).*
**Question 34**
The synovial variety of the Atlanto‑occipital joint is:
A. Pivot
B. Plane
C. Ellipsoid
D. Hinge
**Correct Answer: C. Ellipsoid.**
*Explanation: The atlanto‑occipital joint is a condylar (ellipsoid) synovial joint.*
**Question 35**
During clinical examination of the mouth of a 45‑year‑old man, deviation of the uvula to right side was noticed. What is the cranial nerve mostly affected?
A. Right vagus
B. Left vagus
C. Right glossopharyngeal
D. Left glossopharyngeal
**Correct Answer: B. Left vagus.**
*Explanation: The uvula deviates away from the side of the lesion; left vagus lesion → uvula deviates to the right.*
**Question 36**
The parasympathetic nucleus supplying the lacrimal gland is:
A. Superior salivary
B. Inferior salivary
C. Edinger‑Westphal
D. Dorsal nucleus of vagus
**Correct Answer: A. Superior salivary.**
*Explanation: The superior salivatory nucleus gives rise to preganglionic fibers that reach the lacrimal gland via the greater petrosal nerve.*
**Question 37**
Which of the following structure passes in the gap below the inferior constrictor muscle of the pharynx?
A. Pharyngotympanic tube.
B. Stylopharyngeus muscle.
C. Recurrent laryngeal nerve.
D. External laryngeal nerve.
**Correct Answer: C. Recurrent laryngeal nerve.**
*Explanation: The recurrent laryngeal nerve enters the larynx below the inferior constrictor (between it and the esophagus).*
**Question 38**
The middle meningeal vessels pass through which of the following skull foramina?
A. Ovale
B. Spinosum
C. Lacrurn
D. Rotundum
**Correct Answer: B. Spinosum.**
*Explanation: The middle meningeal artery passes through the foramen spinosum.*
**Question 39**
What is the muscle responsible for flexion of the head to the same side and rotation of the face to the opposite side?
A. Trapezius
B. Sternomastoid
C. Platysma
D. Sternohyoid
**Correct Answer: B. Sternomastoid.**
*Explanation: The sternocleidomastoid flexes the head ipsilaterally and rotates the face contralaterally.*
**Question 40**
A 35‑year‑old man came to the outpatient clinic complaining of dripping of saliva and accumulation of the food in the vestibule of the mouth cavity. Which muscle was most probably affected?
A. Temporalis
B. Masseter
C. Buccinator
D. Lateral pterygoid
**Correct Answer: C. Buccinator.**
*Explanation: The buccinator keeps the vestibule clear; its paralysis causes food accumulation in the cheek.*
**Question 41**
A 55‑year‑old man is admitted to the hospital with severe scalp lacerations, which were sutured. After three days the wound is inflamed, swollen, and painful. Which of the following layers of the scalp is the dangerous area?
A. Skin
B. Connective tissue
C. Aponeurosis of occipitofrontalis
D. Loose areolar tissue
**Correct Answer: D. Loose areolar tissue.**
*Explanation: Infection in the loose areolar tissue (dangerous area) can spread to the cranial cavity via emissary veins.*
**Question 42**
The muscle which rotates the eyeball downwards and laterally is:
A. Superior rectus
B. Inferior rectus
C. Superior oblique
D. Lateral rectus
**Correct Answer: C. Superior oblique.**
*Explanation: The superior oblique primarily intorts and depresses, but also abducts (down and out).*
**Question 43**
A 38‑year‑old man was admitted to the hospital for a partial thyroidectomy. After the operation, the patient could not abduct the true vocal cords due to a nerve injury. Which of the following muscles was most likely affected?
A. Posterior cricoarytenoid
B. Lateral cricoarytenoid
C. Thyroarytenoid
D. Cricothyroid
**Correct Answer: A. Posterior cricoarytenoid.**
*Explanation: The posterior cricoarytenoid is the only abductor of the vocal cords; supplied by the recurrent laryngeal nerve.*
**Question 44**
The parotid duct opens into the vestibule of the mouth opposite:
A. The upper 2nd premolar tooth.
B. The upper 1st molar tooth.
C. The upper 2nd molar tooth.
D. The upper 3rd molar tooth
**Correct Answer: C. The upper 2nd molar tooth.**
*Explanation: The parotid duct opens opposite the upper second molar.*
**Question 45**
The lesser palatine nerve originates from:
A. Maxillary nerve
B. Greater palatine nerve
C. Otic ganglion
D. Sphenopalatine ganglion
**Correct Answer: D. Sphenopalatine ganglion.**
*Explanation: The lesser palatine nerve is a branch of the pterygopalatine ganglion (sphenopalatine ganglion).*
**Question 46**
The only tributary of the subclavian vein is:
A. IJV
B. EJV
C. Brachiocephalic
D. Anterior jugular
**Correct Answer: B. EJV.**
*Explanation: The external jugular vein is a tributary of the subclavian vein.*
**Question 47**
The anterior boundary of tonsillar fossa is:
A. Palatoglossal fold
B. Palatopharyngeal fold
C. Soft palate
D. Dorsum of the tongue
**Correct Answer: A. Palatoglossal fold.**
*Explanation: The palatoglossal arch (anterior pillar) forms the anterior boundary of the tonsillar fossa.*
**Question 48**
In case of right corticobulbar tract injury above the level of the motor nucleus of facial nerve, which side of the face is affected?
A. Upper ½ right side.
B. Lower ½ right side.
C. Upper ½ left side.
D. Lower ½ left side.
**Correct Answer: D. Lower ½ left side.**
*Explanation: UMN lesion of right corticobulbar tract → contralateral lower face weakness (left lower face).*
**Question 49**
Which of the following structures has an extrapyramidal function?
A. Amygdaloid nucleus
B. Claustrum
C. Corpus striatum
D. Fornix
**Correct Answer: C. Corpus striatum.**
*Explanation: The corpus striatum (caudate + putamen) is part of the basal ganglia (extrapyramidal system).*
**Question 50**
The following are projection fibers of the cerebral hemisphere:
A. Corticobulbar
B. Cingulum
C. Arcuate
D. Uncinate
**Correct Answer: A. Corticobulbar.**
*Explanation: Corticobulbar fibers are projection fibers (cortex to brainstem).*
**Question 51**
A 69‑year‑old woman was brought to the ER with inability to speak. She was diagnosed that she had motor aphasia. Which lobe of the brain is affected?
A. Frontal
B. Parietal
C. Temporal
D. Occipital
**Correct Answer: A. Frontal.**
*Explanation: Broca’s area (motor speech) is in the inferior frontal gyrus.*
**Question 52**
The part of the cerebellum concerned with planning and programming movement is:
A. Spinocerebellar
B. Vestibulocerebellum
C. Archicerebellum
D. Neocerebellum
**Correct Answer: D. Neocerebellum.**
*Explanation: The neocerebellum (cerebrocerebellum) is involved in planning and coordination of voluntary movements.*
**Question 53**
A patient came to ER presenting with headache; fever; nausea; stiffness of the neck. He was diagnosed as meningitis. What is the name of the procedure by which a special needle is inserted into the subarachnoid space to get sample of CSF to confirm the diagnosis?
A. Conus medullaris
B. Lumbar cistern
C. Lumbar puncture
D. Filum terminale
**Correct Answer: C. Lumbar puncture.**
*Explanation: Lumbar puncture is performed to obtain CSF from the subarachnoid space.*
**Question 54**
The skin over buccinator is supplied by the following nerve:
a) Ophthalmic
b) Mandibular
c) Maxillary
d) Facial
**Correct Answer: b) Mandibular.**
*Explanation: The buccal branch of the mandibular nerve (V3) supplies skin over the buccinator.*
**Question 55**
The parasympathetic supply of the parotid gland is from the following nerve:
a) Lesser petrosal
b) Deep petrosal
c) Greater petrosal
d) External petrosal
**Correct Answer: a) Lesser petrosal.**
*Explanation: The lesser petrosal nerve (from the tympanic plexus) carries preganglionic parasympathetics to the otic ganglion for the parotid gland.*
**Question 56**
Which of the following lymph nodes drains directly the tip of the tongue?
a) Jugulo‑digastric
b) Jugulo‑omohyoid
c) Submental
d) Submandibular
**Correct Answer: c) Submental.**
*Explanation: The tip of the tongue drains to the submental lymph nodes.*
**Question 57**
The only infrahyoid muscle which is not supplied by ansa cervicalis is:
a) Sternohyoid
b) Thyrohyoid
c) Omohyoid
d) Sternothyroid
**Correct Answer: b) Thyrohyoid.**
*Explanation: Thyrohyoid is supplied by C1 via the hypoglossal nerve, not by ansa cervicalis.*
**Question 58**
The rootlets of the vagus nerve are emerging between:
a) Pyramid and olive.
b) Pyramid and pons.
c) Olive and inferior cerebellar peduncle.
d) Gracile and cuneate tubercles.
**Correct Answer: c) Olive and inferior cerebellar peduncle.**
*Explanation: Vagus rootlets emerge from the medulla in the post‑olivary sulcus (between olive and inferior cerebellar peduncle).*
**Question 59**
The part of the cerebellum concerned with balance is:
a) Paleocerebellum
b) Neocerebellum
c) Flocculonodular
d) Lateral zone area
**Correct Answer: c) Flocculonodular.**
*Explanation: The flocculonodular lobe (vestibulocerebellum) is concerned with balance and eye movements.*
**Question 60**
Deep inguinal ring is a gap in:
a) Fascia transversalis
b) External spermatic fascia
c) External oblique aponeurosis
d) Cremasteric muscle
**Correct Answer: a) Fascia transversalis.**
*Explanation: The deep inguinal ring is an opening in the transversalis fascia.*
**Question 61**
The short gastric arteries originate from:
a) Hepatic
b) Coeliac
c) Gastroduodenal
d) Splenic
**Correct Answer: d) Splenic.**
*Explanation: Short gastric arteries are branches of the splenic artery.*
**Question 62**
The bare area of the liver is enclosed by which of the following ligaments:
a) ligamentum teres
b) Coronary ligament
c) Ligamentum venosum
d) Falciform ligament
**Correct Answer: b) Coronary ligament.**
*Explanation: The bare area of the liver is bounded by the two layers of the coronary ligament.*
**Question 63**
Which of the following nerves emerges from the medial aspect of psoas major muscle?
a) Obturator
b) Iliohypogastric
c) Ilioinguinal
d) Lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh
**Correct Answer: a) Obturator.**
*Explanation: The obturator nerve emerges from the medial border of psoas major.*
**Question 64**
The following single branch of abdominal aorta arises at the level of L3:
a) Coeliac
b) Superior mesenteric
c) Inferior mesenteric
d) Median sacral
**Correct Answer: d) Median sacral.**
*Explanation: The median sacral artery arises from the aorta just above its bifurcation (L4 level usually, but among options it is the most caudal).*
**Question 65**
The lateral umbilical fold is raised by:
a) Obliterated umbilical artery
b) Obliterated urachus
c) Deep circumflex iliac artery
d) Inferior epigastric artery
**Correct Answer: d) Inferior epigastric artery.**
*Explanation: The lateral umbilical fold contains the inferior epigastric vessels.*
**Question 66**
A 68 year‑old man with forward tilting of the trunk and short steps, rigidity and trembling. Diagnosis: Parkinson’s disease. Which area of the midbrain is suspected to be affected?
a. Crus cerebri
b. Substantia nigra
c. Tegmentum
d. Tectum
**Correct Answer: b. Substantia nigra.**
*Explanation: Parkinson’s disease involves degeneration of the substantia nigra pars compacta (dopaminergic neurons).*
**Question 67**
A 64‑year‑old man unable to produce speech but understands and can write words. What area is affected?
a. Broca's area
b. Premotor area
c. Wernicke's area
d. Supplementary motor area
**Correct Answer: a. Broca's area.**
*Explanation: Broca’s aphasia (non‑fluent) affects speech production with preserved comprehension.*
**Question 68**
A 45‑year‑old male with absent cremasteric reflex. Which nerve is responsible for the efferent limb?
a) Ilioinguinal
b) Iliohypogastric
c) Genitofemoral
d) Pudendal
**Correct Answer: c) Genitofemoral.**
*Explanation: The cremasteric reflex efferent limb is via the genital branch of the genitofemoral nerve.*
**Question 69**
A 70‑year‑old man with 90% blockage at the origin of the inferior mesenteric artery. Which artery would provide collateral supply to the descending colon?
a) Left gastroepiploic
b) Middle colic artery
c) Sigmoid artery
d) Superior rectal artery
**Correct Answer: b) Middle colic artery.**
*Explanation: The middle colic artery (from superior mesenteric) can collateralize with the left colic artery (from inferior mesenteric).*
**Question 70**
A 55‑year‑old man with a perforating ulcer in the posterior wall of the stomach. Where would peritonitis most likely develop initially?
a) Omental bursa
b) Greater sac
c) Morison's pouch
d) Right subhepatic space
**Correct Answer: a) Omental bursa.**
*Explanation: Posterior gastric ulcer perforates into the lesser sac (omental bursa).*
