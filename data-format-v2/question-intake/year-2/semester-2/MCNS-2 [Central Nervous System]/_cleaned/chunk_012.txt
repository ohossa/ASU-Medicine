LECTURE: N/A

CASE_TEXT:
A patient opened his mouth widely while yawning, after which he could not close his mouth.
SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) [TYPE: essay] Name the joint affected & its type.
   MODEL_ANSWER: Temporomandibular joint - synovial condylar.
2) [TYPE: essay] List the ligaments of the joint. Which one of them is important functionally?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Lateral temporomandibular ligament (most important functionally), stylomandibular ligament & sphenomandibular ligament.
3) [TYPE: essay] Mention the nerve supply of the joint.
   MODEL_ANSWER: Auriculotemporal & masseteric nerves.

N/A

QUESTION_TYPE: case
SUBJECT: Anatomy
CHAPTER: Head and Neck Anatomy
LECTURE: N/A

CASE_TEXT:
During surgical removal of a tumor in the infratemporal fossa, an intense hemorrhage occurred. The surgeon clamped the main source of the arterial supply to the area.
SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) [TYPE: essay] Which artery did the surgeon clamp to stop the hemorrhage?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Maxillary artery.
2) [TYPE: essay] Name the main nerve located in the fossa & its branches.
   MODEL_ANSWER: Mandibular nerve.
3) [TYPE: essay] After the operation, the patient complained of loss of taste sensation from anterior 2/3 of tongue. What is your explanation for this case. Name the nucleus that receives the taste sensation.
   MODEL_ANSWER: Injury of lingual nerve (which contains fibers of chorda tympani) - solitary nucleus.

N/A
