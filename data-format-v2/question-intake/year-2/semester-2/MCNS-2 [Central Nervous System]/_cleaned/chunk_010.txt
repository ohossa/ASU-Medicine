   TEXT: Define the venous sinus & its function.
   MODEL_ANSWER:

2) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Describe the site & the contents of the cavernous sinus.
   MODEL_ANSWER:
   Site: in middle cranial fossa on each side of body of sphenoid. Its contents are internal carotid artery & abducent nerve.

   MODEL_ANSWER:
   The dangerous area is the triangular area of face that extends between medial angles of eyes & lateral angles of mouth. It includes upper lip, nose & medial angles of eyes. It is dangerous because infection of this area can reach the cavernous sinus causing its thrombosis.

   Refer to tributaries & communications of cavernous sinus in text book.

CASE_TEXT:
A 20 year old student was playing baseball when the ball hit him on the side of the head. He immediately fell to the ground unconscious. After 30 seconds he felt well & decided to sit & watch the match. An hour later, he felt so sleepy & then he became unconscious again. He was taken to the hospital & a skull X-ray showed a fracture in the region of the pterion.

SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Define the pterion.
   MODEL_ANSWER:
   It is the center of H-shaped sutures between 4 bones: frontal, parietal, temporal & greater wing of sphenoid.
2) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Why are fractures in this region extremely dangerous?
   MODEL_ANSWER:
   Because deep to it lies anterior branch of middle meningeal artery that may be cut causing extradural hemorrhage that may press on motor area of the cerebral hemisphere.
