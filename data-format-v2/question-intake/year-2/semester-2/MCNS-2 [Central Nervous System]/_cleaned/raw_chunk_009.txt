Casel. A 60 year old female complained of a sudden disturbance in gait, 
intension tremors, ptosis of right eyelid, decreased sweating on right

Answers of Cases 

* Answers of Case 1: 

a. Occlusion of right posterior inferior cerebellar artery. 
b. Analysis of symptoms: 

i. Disturbance of gait & intension tremors > due to lesion of 
spinocerebellar tract & ICP. 
ii. Ptosis of right eyelid + decreased sweating - due to ipsilateral 

Horner's syndrome -> due to lesion of descending sympathetic fibers. 
iii. Decreased pain sensation of right side of face - due to lesion of 
spinal N & tract of V. 
iv. Difficulty in swallowing = due to lesion of nucleus ambiguus > 
paralysis of muscles of palate, pharynx & larynx. 
v. Decreased pain sensation in left side of body = due to lesion of spinal 
lemniscus - contralateral loss of pain & temperature from body. 

* Answers of Case 2: 

a. Cranial nerves affected: Facial & vestibule-cochlear nerves. 

b. Compression of facial nerve -> facial paralysis & compression of vestibule- 
cochlear nerve > deafness & vertigo. 

* Answers of Case 3: 

a. Lateral medullary syndrome. 
b. Left posterior inferior cerebellar artery. 
¢. Nucleus ambiguus. 
d. Due to affection of ipsilateral spinal nucleus of trigeminal nerve. 
e. Due to affection of left spinal lemniscus which carries pain & temperature 

from opposite side of body.
