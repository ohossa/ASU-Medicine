Cases 

Case 1: An old man starts to develop headache and by MRI proved to have 
tumors of the medial geniculate body. 
a. Where is the medial geniculate body located? 
b. Where do its efferent fibers project to? 

* Answers of Case 1: 
a. Located below the posterior end of the thalamus. 
b. Its efferents form the auditory radiation which projects directly to the 
auditory area of cerebral cortex.
