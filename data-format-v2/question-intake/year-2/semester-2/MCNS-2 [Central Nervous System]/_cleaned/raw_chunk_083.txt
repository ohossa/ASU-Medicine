## Head and Neck Questions

**1.** The occipitofrontalis muscle accounts for the ……… layer of the scalp.  
A. First  
B. Second  
C. Third  
D. Fourth  
**Answer: C**

**2.** Hemorrhage reaching the ……… layer of the scalp can cause black eye.  
A. Dense connective tissue  
B. Aponeurotic  
C. Loose connective tissue  
D. Periosteal  
**Answer: C**

**3.** Skin of the hairy area of the temple is supplied by ……… nerve.  
A. Infraorbital  
B. Zygomaticotemporal  
C. Auriculotemporal  
D. Great auricular  
**Answer: C**

**4.** The great auricular nerve carries fibers from:  
A. C2 and C3 ventral rami.  
B. C2 ventral ramus.  
C. C2 dorsal ramus.  
D. C3 dorsal ramus.  
**Answer: A**

**5.** Skin over occipital bone up to the vertex is supplied by:  
A. C2 ventral ramus.  
B. C3 ventral ramus.  
C. C2 dorsal ramus.  
D. C3 dorsal ramus.  
**Answer: C**

**6.** The anterior facial vein is formed by union of ……… veins.  
A. Supratrochlear and supraorbital  
B. Superficial temporal and maxillary  
C. Posterior auricular and occipital  
D. Supraorbital and infraorbital  
**Answer: A**

**7.** The common facial vein drains into the ……… vein.  
A. Retromandibular  
B. External jugular  
C. Internal jugular  
D. Subclavian  
**Answer: C**

**8.** A muscle covered by deep fascia is:  
A. Orbicularis oculi.  
B. Orbicularis oris.  
C. Masseter.  
D. Buccinator.  
**Answer: D**

**9.** A muscle develops from the first pharyngeal arch is:  
A. Occipitofrontalis.  
B. Platysma.  
C. Buccinator.  
D. Masseter.  
**Answer: D**

**10.** Choose the wrong statement about the orbicularis oculi muscle:  
A. Its orbital part closes the eye tightly.  
B. Its palpebral part closes the eye gently.  
C. Its lacrimal part is attached in front of the lacrimal sac.  
D. It develops from the second pharyngeal arch.  
**Answer: C**

**11.** An active muscle during whistling is:  
A. Orbicularis oris.  
B. Buccinator.  
C. Masseter.  
D. Platysma.  
**Answer: A**

**12.** Accumulation of food in the vestibule of the mouth denotes weakness of ……… muscle.  
A. Buccinator  
B. Masseter  
C. Medial pterygoid  
D. Lateral pterygoid  
**Answer: A**

**13.** Skin over the angle of the mandible is supplied by ……… nerve.  
A. Auriculotemporal  
B. Great auricular  
C. Posterior auricular

**Answer: B**

**14.** Pain from the lower eyelid is carried by ……… nerve.  
A. Supratrochlear  
B. Supraorbital  
C. Infratrochlear

**Answer: D**

**15.** Infection in the area of chin leads to enlargement of ……… lymph nodes.  
A. Pre‑auricular  
B. Posterior auricular  
C. Submandibular  
D. Submental  
**Answer: D**

**16.** The dural fold which is attached to the skull vault is:  
A. Falx cerebri.  
B. Falx cerebelli.  
C. Tentorium cerebelli.  
D. Diaphragm sellae.  
**Answer: A**

**17.** Ethmoidal arteries supply the cranial dura via ……… arteries.  
A. Anterior meningeal  
B. Middle meningeal  
C. Accessory meningeal  
D. Posterior meningeal

**18.** All the following are true about the middle meningeal artery EXCEPT:

C. It enters the cranial cavity through foramen ovale.

**21.** The cavernous sinus is drained to the transverse sinus by ……… sinus.

**22.** Choose the wrong statement about the straight sinus:

**24.** Which of the following is true about the intracranial hemorrhage?
