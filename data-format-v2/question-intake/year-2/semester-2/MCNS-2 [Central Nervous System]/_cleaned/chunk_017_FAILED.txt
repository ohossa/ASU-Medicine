Answers of Cases 

* Answer of Case 1: 

a. Right hemisphere. 
b. Anterior cerebral artery. 
c. Since the speech is normal, so the lesion is not in the dominant hemisphere 
(which is the left hemisphere, if the patient is right-sided). Since the affection 
of the lower limb is more, this indicates the affection of the paracentral lobule 

(which lies on the medial surface of the hemisphere & is supplied by the 
anterior cerebral artery). 
d. Arises from internal carotid artery.

a. Left middle cerebral artery due to the affection of the lower part of the 
precentral gyrus (which is supplied by the middle cerebral artery). Affection of 
the right limb indicates a lesion in the left hemisphere.

c. Central branches of this artery supplies posterior parts of caudate &

* Answers of Case 4:

middle temporal gyri. Its function is understanding auditory & visual 
information.
