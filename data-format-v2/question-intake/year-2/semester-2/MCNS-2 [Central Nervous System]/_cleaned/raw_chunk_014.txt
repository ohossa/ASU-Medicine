Answers of Cases 

* Case 1: a. Hypoglossal nerve. b. Left side. 
¢c. Through hypoglossal foramen. 

* Case 2: a. left sternomastoid. b. Spinal accessory nerve. 
c. Investing cervical deep fascia. 

* Case 3: a. Cranial accessory nerve which supplies the muscles of the 
pharynx (responsible for swallowing). b. Inferior petrosal sinus. 

* Case 4: 

a. Test the integrity of the vagus nerve by examining the palatal arches 
on both sides. In case of paralysis > there is no arching & the 
uvula is deviated to the normal side. 

b. Sensory branches of glossopharyngeal nerve = tympanic, carotid, 

tonsillar & lingual. 
c. Muscles supplied by accessory nerve: 

i. spinal accessory > sternomastoid & trapezius. 
ii. cranial accessory - muscles of palate, pharynx & larynx.
