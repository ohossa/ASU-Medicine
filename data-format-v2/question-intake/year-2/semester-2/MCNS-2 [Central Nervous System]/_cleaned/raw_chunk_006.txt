Answers of Cases 

Answer of Case 1: 
a. UMNL in part of area 4 of left hemisphere. 
b. Lower %* of 7 cranial nerve nucleus & 12™ cranial nerve nucleus receive

cranial nerve nuclei receive bilateral cortico-nuclear tract, so they are

c. Area 4 controls voluntary movements of opposite side of body. Body is
