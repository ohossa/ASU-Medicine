After 30 seconds he felt well & decided to sit & watch the match. 
An hour later, he felt so sleepy & then he became unconscious again.

b. Why are fractures in this region extremely dangerous? 

Answers of Cases 

* Answers of Case 1: 

a. Venous sinuses are dilated venous channels situated between dural layers. Its 
functions include: i. Venous drainage of brain, skull & meninges. 

ii. Equalization of venous pressure outside & inside skull. 
iii. Drainage of CSF. 

b. Site: in middle cranial fossa on each side of body of sphenoid. Its contents 
are internal carotid artery & abducent nerve. 
¢c. The dangerous area is the triangular area of face that extends between 
medial angles of eyes & lateral angles of mouth. It includes upper lip, nose & 
medial angles of eyes. It is dangerous because infection of this area can reach 

the cavernous sinus causing its thrombosis. 
d. Refer to tributaries & communications of cavernous sinus in text book. 

* Answers of Case 2: 

a. It is the center of H-shaped sutures between 4 bones: frontal, parietal, 

temporal & greater wing of sphenoid. 
b. Because deep to it lies anterior branch of middle meningeal artery that may 
be cut causing extradural hemorrhage that may press on motor area of the 

cerebral hemisphere.
