CASE_TEXT:
A patient with an abscess in the upper lip was advised not to squeeze it for fear of spread of pus to a venous sinus inside skull.

SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) TYPE: mcq
   TEXT: What is the name of sinus at risk?
   ANSWER: Cavernous sinus.
2) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Describe 2 alternate pathways for passage of infection to the sinus.
   MODEL_ANSWER: First pathway: Facial vein - ophthalmic vein -> cavernous sinus. Second pathway: Facial vein - deep facial vein = pterygoid plexus of veins emissary veins -> cavernous sinus.
3) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: What other areas that have similar risk?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Medial angle of eye, nose & cheecks.

CHAPTER: Scalp
LECTURE: N/A

CASE_TEXT:
A D years old child fell off his bicycle & his head hit the pavement. A 2 cm long wound in his scalp was seen. Soon, he was sitting in a pool of blood.

SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Why does the scalp bleed profusely?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Because the scalp has a rich blood supply & the fibrous tissue in the connective tissue layer prevents the local vasoconstriction of the blood vessels in this layer.
2) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Name the arteries that supply the scalp.
   MODEL_ANSWER: * Three arteries in front of auricle: supratrochlear, supraorbital & Superficial temporal arteries. * Two arteries behind the auricle: posterior auricular & occipital arteries.
3) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Which is the dangerous layer of the scalp? Why is it dangerous?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Loose areolar tissue layer because it contains the emissary veins that are valveless & can take infection to the inside of the skull.

QUESTION_TYPE: case
SUBJECT: Anatomy
CHAPTER: Scalp
LECTURE: N/A

CASE_TEXT:
A 12-years old boy fell from a bicycle & wounded his scalp. At the hospital, the doctor sutured the wound to limit its gaping & to stop the bleeding. Later, the boy returned to the hospital with black eye. The doctor prescribed an antibiotic to avoid dangerous complications.

SUB_QUESTIONS:
1) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Why scalp bleeding cannot be stopped except by suturing?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Because the scalp has a rich blood supply & the fibrous tissue in the connective tissue layer prevents the local vasoconstriction of the blood vessels in this layer.
2) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Gaping of scalp wound indicates involvement of which layer of scalp?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Gaping of scalp wound indicates involvement of aponeurosis of occipito-frontalis muscle.
3) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: Explain the development of black eye.
   MODEL_ANSWER: If the wound causes subaponeurotic hematoma -> the blood gravitates the the eyelids deep to the frontal bellies of the occipito-frontalis muscle which are attached to the skin of eyebrows & so hematoma extends deep to it & reaches eyelids causing black eye.
4) TYPE: essay
   TEXT: What dangerous complication did the doctor expect?
   MODEL_ANSWER: Cavernous sinus thrombosis.
