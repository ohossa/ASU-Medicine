Answers of Cases 

* Answers of Case 1: 

a. Cavernous sinus. 

b. First pathway: Facial vein - ophthalmic vein -> cavernous sinus. 
Second pathway: Facial vein - deep facial vein = pterygoid plexus of veins 

emissary veins -> cavernous sinus. 

c. Medial angle of eye, nose & cheecks. 

* Answers of Case 2: 

a. Because the scalp has a rich blood supply & the fibrous tissue in the 
connective tissue layer prevents the local vasoconstriction of the blood vessels 
in this layer. 

b. * Three arteries in front of auricle: supratrochlear, supraorbital & 
Superficial temporal arteries. 

* Two arteries behind the auricle: posterior auricular & occipital arteries. 

c. Loose areolar tissue layer because it contains the emissary veins that are 
valveless & can take infection to the inside of the skull. 

* Answers of Case 3: 

a. Because the scalp has a rich blood supply & the fibrous tissue in the 

connective tissue layer prevents the local vasoconstriction of the blood vessels 
in this layer. 
b. Gaping of scalp wound indicates involvement of aponeurosis of occipito- 
frontalis muscle. 
c. If the wound causes subaponeurotic hematoma -> the blood gravitates the 
the eyelids deep to the frontal bellies of the occipito-frontalis muscle which are 
attached to the skin of eyebrows & so hematoma extends deep to it & reaches 
eyelids causing black eye. 

d. Cavernous sinus thrombosis.
