Cases 

Case 1. A patient opened his mouth widely while yawning, after which he 
could not close his mouth. 
a. Name the joint affected & its type. 
b. List the ligaments of the joint. Which one of them is important 

functionally? 
c. Mention the nerve supply of the joint. 

Case 2. During surgical removal of a tumor in the infratemporal fossa, an 
intense hemorrhage occurred. The surgeon clamped the main source 
of the arterial supply to the area. 
a. Which artery dis the surgeon clamp to stop the hemorrhage? 
b. Name the main nerve located in the fossa & its branches. 
c. After the operation, the patient complained of loss of taste sensation 

from anterior 2/3 of tongue. What is your explanation for this case. 
Name the nucleus that receives the taste sensation. 

Answers of Cases 

* Answers of Case 1: 
a. Temporo-mandibular joint - synovial condylar. 
b. Lateral temporo-mandibular ligament (most important functionally), 

stylomandibular ligament & sphenomandibular ligament. 
c. Auriculo-temporal & masseteric nerves. 

* Answers of Case 2: 
a. Maxillary artery. 
b. Mandibular nerve. 
c. Injury of lingual nerve (which contains fibers of chorda tympani) - solitary 

nucleus.
