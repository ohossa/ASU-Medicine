Anatomy Wrap Up

**Question 1**  
A 25 year old woman suffers from a traumatic scalp avulsion due to a revolving machinery at her workplace. All the following are considered boundaries for the scalp EXCEPT:  
A. Superior nuchal line  
B. Zygomatic arch  
C. Zygomatic bone  
D. Supraorbital arch  
E. External occipital protuberance  

**Correct Answer: C. Zygomatic bone**  
*Explanation: The scalp boundaries include the superior nuchal line, zygomatic arch, supraorbital arch, and external occipital protuberance, but not the zygomatic bone.*

---

**Question 2**  
A 30-year-old patient comes to the emergency room with a laceration on the scalp following a head injury. The wound appears to be bleeding profusely. Which layer of the scalp is primarily responsible for this bleeding?  
A. Skin  
B. Subcutaneous tissue (Superficial Fascia)  
C. Galea Aponeurotica (Epicranial Aponeurosis)  
D. Loose Connective Tissue  
E. Pericranium (Periosteum)  

**Correct Answer: B. Subcutaneous tissue (Superficial Fascia)**  
*Explanation: The subcutaneous tissue (superficial fascia) of the scalp is highly vascular and contains many blood vessels, leading to profuse bleeding when injured.*

---

**Question 3**  
A 30-year-old patient presents with a painful, swollen area on their scalp. Upon examination, you notice a localized, raised, and red lesion in the frontal region. The patient reports tenderness when they comb or brush their hair. Which layer of the scalp is most likely affected in this case?  
A. Skin  
B. Subcutaneous tissue  
C. Galea aponeurotica (epicranial aponeurosis)  
D. Loose connective tissue (areolar tissue)  
E. Periosteum  

**Correct Answer: B. Subcutaneous tissue**  
*Explanation: The subcutaneous layer contains nerves and vessels; inflammation here causes pain and tenderness (e.g., folliculitis).*

**Question 4**  
A 25-year-old patient sustains a head injury during a bicycle accident. On examination, there is a palpable swelling on the scalp that appears to be filled with blood. The hematoma is located between the periosteum and the galea aponeurotica. Which specific type of scalp hematoma is this most likely indicative of?  
A. Epidural hematoma  
B. Subdural hematoma  
C. Subperiosteal hematoma  
D. Subcutaneous hematoma  
E. Subgaleal hematoma  

**Correct Answer: E. Subgaleal hematoma**  
*Explanation: A subgaleal hematoma occurs between the galea aponeurotica and the periosteum (pericranium).*

**Question 18**  
A 50-year-old female patient presents to the clinic with a chief complaint of right-sided hemiparesis. She reports that she woke up this morning and was unable to move her right arm or leg. Which of the following anatomical areas of the brain is most likely affected in this patient?

**Question 30**  
A 44-year-old female patient has a lesion in the right cerebral peduncle in the midbrain. She would present with a loss of:  
1. fine touch and pain sensation from the entire left side of the body
