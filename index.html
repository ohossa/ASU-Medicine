Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is A-Plus (asu.codes)?
A-Plus is a free, independent clinical study companion and question bank built by medical student Omar Hossam to assist medical students across Egypt with verified past exam MCQs and clinical spotters.
Which university curricula are covered?
A-Plus covers the integrated system-based modular medical curriculum taught in Egyptian universities, specifically matching module codes and syllabi for Years 1–3 at Ain Shams University Faculty of Medicine.
