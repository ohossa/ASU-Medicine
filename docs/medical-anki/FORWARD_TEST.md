## Scenario 2: analgesic nephropathy wholefigure

Actual asset: `Urogenital_Anki_Project/media/book_recrops/final_pathology_gap/Netter_P4_30_analgesic_nephropathy_300dpi.png`, 1630×1225 pixels, SHA-256 `b408546aab9377f4a9b6e3d5238f4f87cc6ffcf96fe194500537e80f00070d65`.

Viewed the complete figure, including peripheral text and signatures. Read title “Analgesic nephropathy”; possible pathogenetic mechanisms; phenacetin; accumulation of toxic metabolites in the kidneys; low papillary blood flow/oxygen tension and lack of reducing agents; a large cut kidney labelled necrosis and sloughing of renal papillae; slowly progressive renal insufficiency; flank pain; haematuria/mild proteinuria; passage of blood and tissue into urine. The key conflict is visible text ending “prostaglandin-induced vasoconstriction.” Source signatures/credit are present and must not be erased.

The kidney illustration visibly supports papillary necrosis/sloughing and passage of tissue. It is a schematic gross illustration, not histological evidence identifying coagulative necrosis. Therefore even a safe isolated panel would provide only partial support for a card asking for both sloughing and coagulative necrosis.

Investigated the mechanism uncertainty using primary experimental evidence. Elliott et al., Life Sciences 1986, PMID 3736334, DOI 10.1016/0024-3205(86)90522-9, reports that a PGE2 analogue reduced mefenamic-acid-associated papillary necrosis in rats, consistent with prostaglandin depletion and protection rather than the unqualified mechanism printed in the diagram. Opened the PubMed abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3736334/. Also retrieved the primary rat COX study PMID 9502396, whose abstract describes inhibition of renal vasodilatory prostaglandins/secondary ischaemia as a proposed mechanism: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9502396/ (search returned the abstract; direct open returned only a disclosure page). These animal results do not prove every prostanoid effect in every clinical circumstance; the issue is the figure's unqualified mechanism in this context. No new tested medical facts were inserted into a card.

Decision: reject the supplied wholefigure for attachment; record the visible-text conflict and evidence. Do not mask or rewrite the label. Prefer a correct alternative. A source-faithful isolated kidney panel is only a candidate if its title/identity, authentic explanatory text, arrows and required credit remain complete and there are no misleading cut connectors; review it anew and record its limited gross-morphology coverage. Do not declare the current wholefigure acceptable merely because the right-hand kidney matches the card. Actual source-page provenance/edition and reuse clearance remain unchecked in this test.
