## 2. Directory Architecture & Naming Conventions

Every folder follows an alphanumeric ordering strategy (`00.` prefixes for resource folders, followed by numbered subject folders) so that iPadOS Files and Goodnotes always sort your material in the exact chronological sequence taught at Ain Shams.

### Standard Structure Inside Every Module:
```text
Medicine ASU/
└── Year X (XX CP)/
    └── Semester Y/
        └── [MODULE-CODE] Module Name/
            ├── 00. Textbooks/                      <-- Reference textbooks & atlases
            ├── 00. SGDs (Small Group Discussions)/ <-- Departmental case booklets & problem sets
            ├── 01. Subject A/                      <-- Lecture slides & notes
            ├── 02. Subject B/
            └── ...
```

## 4. How to Import and Use on Your iPad

### Step 1: Transfer the ZIP Archive to Your iPad
Choose the easiest method for your setup:
* **Option A (AirDrop — Recommended)**:
  1. In macOS Finder, navigate to `/Users/omarhossa/Documents/Medcine ASU/ASU-Medical-Portal/dist/Goodnotes_Organization/`.
  2. Right-click `Medicine_ASU_Goodnotes_Structure.zip` > **Share** > **AirDrop** > select your **iPad**.
  3. On your iPad, tap **Save to Files** and choose either **iCloud Drive** or **On My iPad**.
* **Option B (iCloud Drive Direct)**:
  Copy the ZIP file (or the unzipped `Medicine ASU` folder) directly into your iCloud Drive folder on your Mac. It will sync automatically to the Files app on your iPad.

---

### Step 2: Unzip on iPad
1. Open the built-in **Files app** on your iPad.
2. Locate `Medicine_ASU_Goodnotes_Structure.zip`.
3. Simply **tap the ZIP file once**.
4. iPadOS will immediately extract the pristine `Medicine ASU` folder containing all 5 years, semesters, modules, textbooks, SGDs, and subjects!

---

### Step 3: Best Ways to Use with Goodnotes on iPad

Goodnotes is designed to work in synergy with the iPadOS Files app:

#### Method A: Split View Drag & Drop (Fastest & Most Natural for iPad)
1. Open **Goodnotes** on your iPad.
2. Swipe up from the bottom of your screen to reveal the iPad Dock.
3. Drag the **Files app** icon to the right side of the screen to open **Split View** (Goodnotes on the left, Files on the right).
4. In Goodnotes, navigate to your desired year or create a top-level `Medicine ASU` folder once.
5. In the Files app, open the specific module or subject folder where you keep your downloaded PDF lecture slides or SGD booklets.
6. **Drag and drop** your lecture slides directly from Files into Goodnotes! Goodnotes opens them instantly as handwritten digital notebooks.

#### Method B: Direct Import from Inside Goodnotes
1. Inside Goodnotes, open the module folder you are currently studying.
2. Tap the large **`+ New...`** button > **Import**.
3. Browse your Files app to `Medicine ASU/Year X/Semester Y/[Module]/[Subject]`.
4. Select your lecture PDF or note file. It will import directly into that exact location.

#### Method C: Share Sheet Import from Anywhere
1. Whenever a professor uploads a lecture PDF (via Telegram, WhatsApp, ASU SIS, or Safari):
2. Tap the **Share** button > select **Goodnotes**.
3. Goodnotes will ask: *"Import to Current Document"* or *"Import as New Document"*, and lets you pick the exact module/subject folder in your Goodnotes library!

## 5. Pro-Tips for Peak Goodnotes Organization at Ain Shams

1. **Color-Code Your Goodnotes Folder Icons**:
   * Year 1: Blue
   * Year 2: Green
   * Year 3: Yellow
   * Year 4: Orange
   * Year 5: Red
2. **Pin "Textbooks" and "SGDs"**:
   Because every module contains `00. Textbooks` and `00. SGDs`, Goodnotes and Files will always sort them to the top of the list, keeping your core reference material instantly accessible without scrolling.
3. **Goodnotes Auto-Backup**:
   In Goodnotes Settings (`Gear icon` > `Cloud & Backup` > `Auto-Backup`), turn on Auto-Backup to your Google Drive or OneDrive in PDF format. This ensures every note you take is safely preserved and searchable across all devices!
